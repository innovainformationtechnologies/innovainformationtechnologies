# About page

**Innova: The Mission**

We build infrastructure for independent creators and small operators to own their own tools, audience, and income without being dependent on platforms that can change the rules on them anytime.

**Innova: The Philosophy**

Everything is data.

Not metaphorically. Literally. A recipe is a vector of ingredients. A person's day is a matrix of time and intention. A genome is a vector of biological instructions. A game character moving through a 3D world is a transform matrix being multiplied thousands of times per second. The nuclear launch codes sitting in a 100 year old government computer somewhere are being read as a vector whether anyone designed it that way or not.

I figured this out building game engines from scratch in high school. Not because someone assigned it. Because I wanted to understand what was actually happening underneath. When you build a game engine yourself you don't get to pretend that rotation is magic; you have to write the matrix. You have to feel it work. And once you feel it work in one place you start seeing it everywhere else.

My brain never really stopped doing that. Business ideas float around in my head like tables in a database. I look at code and my brain runs it before I hit enter. I'm always running background calculations. It's just how the processor works at this point.

Innova is what happens when you build software from that starting point.

Most frameworks are built around specific use cases. Here's a blog template. Here's an ecommerce template. Here's what we think you need. Innova starts from the other direction: if everything is representable as structured data, then a component that understands how to render structured data is a component that can render almost anything. The spreadsheet isn't a limitation. It's the most familiar data structure most people already use every day. A row is a vector. A sheet is a matrix. Most people are already thinking in Innova's language without knowing it.

The SheetSite framework is my first full expression of that. You give it a spreadsheet, it gives you a website. The component doesn't need to know everything, it only needs to know where things are and what type they are. A gallery component knows it's looking for media files. It finds them, figures out what they are, and spawns the right child to render them. The spreadsheet never gets cluttered with details it doesn't need. Everyone stays in their lane. None of these are original concepts. But most implementations treat it as a proprietary advantage. Innova treats it as an open, extensible standard owned by the people who build on it.

That's the architecture principle underneath everything I build. Simple in Structure. Powerful in Execution. Not as a slogan but as a consequence of how the underlying math works. A matrix can represent a rotation, a color transformation, a recommendation algorithm, a genome comparison, a financial model, and a recipe all with the same fundamental operations. The simplicity isn't a design choice. It's what you arrive at when you understand what's actually happening underneath.

Innova is built for regular people with dreams and aspirations. Not corporations. Not governments. Just people who have something they're trying to do and need tools powerful enough to actually do it without needing a computer science degree to operate them.

Onyx Audio Tech runs on Innova. A 24/7 livestream with segmented programming, automated scheduling, a timelapse highlight system, and a two platform broadcast operation. If that can run on Innova's tools, so can your business, your creative operation, your community platform, your idea that's been sitting in a notes app since 2022.

That's the point. That's always been the point.

If I can do it, you can too.
